What the app calculates

The current app supports the pipe categories shown in its Japanese interface: drainage DV, water-supply TS/HI, and hot-water HT. It calculates the straight pipe cut length for a 45° + 45° connection using the selected category, size and entered centre-to-centre distance.

Why 1.414 appears in 45° offset work

For a simple 45-degree offset, diagonal centre-to-centre travel equals the perpendicular offset multiplied by √2 (approximately 1.4142). The actual pipe cut length is not necessarily the same as travel: fitting dimensions and insertion conditions must also be considered.

Important regional limitation

The app is designed around the Japanese pipe types and millimetre-based values shown in the app. Do not assume support for US/Canadian inch sizes, schedules, fitting standards or other regional systems. Verify the manufacturer’s data and job specifications before fabrication.
